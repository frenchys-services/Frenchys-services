How it works

From your first order to your finished design, here is every step.

  1. 1

    Choose your services

    Browse the services above and tap Add package on everything you want. You can pick more than one, like a logo and a website.

  2. 2

    Send your order

    Open your cart, fill in your name, email and business, and tap Send order. Your order is sent right here on the site, and you’ll see a confirmation when it goes through. No email app needed.

  3. 3

    I confirm the details

    I’ll reply by email, usually within a day, to confirm your packages, the price, and the timeline. I may ask a few questions about your business.

  4. 4

    Agree and pay the deposit

    Once you’re happy with the plan, I’ll email the payment details. Work starts after a deposit of half the price. The rest is due when you approve the final version.

  5. 5

    Send me your materials

    Email me what I need: your logo if you have one, business details, photos, colors you like, and examples of designs you love.

  6. 6

    Review your first draft

    I’ll send a draft for you to look at. Tell me what you like and what you want changed.

  7. 7

    Free revisions

    I’ll update the design based on your feedback until you’re happy. Revisions are free on every package.

  8. 8

    Final delivery

    After you approve the design and the final payment is made, I send you the finished logo files or website files and help you get everything live.

Questions and answers

The things people ask most. Don’t see yours? Email me.

How long does a project take?

I work fast, and I confirm the exact timeline by email before any payment, based on the packages you pick and when you need it. If you have a deadline, add it to your order.

How do revisions work?

Revisions are free on every package. I send a draft, you tell me what to change, and I update it until you’re happy.

When do I pay, and how much?

No payment is taken on this site. After I confirm your plan by email, I send payment details. Work starts after a deposit of half the price, and the rest is due when you approve the final version.

What do I get at the end?

After you approve the design and the final payment is made, I send you the finished logo files or website files and help you get everything live. I’ll confirm exactly which files you need when I email you.

Can I order more than one package?

Yes. Add everything you want to your cart, like a logo and a website. The Logo and Site Bundle costs less than buying both separately.

Do you work with people outside Florida?

Yes. Everything is done online and by email, so I work with clients anywhere.

What should I have ready before I order?

Your business name and what you do, colors and styles you like, your logo or photos if you have them, links to designs you love, and your deadline. You can add all of it in the order form.

Can I ask for something custom?

Yes. Email frenchycollins2010@gmail.com and tell me what you need.
